## Restore sequence

1. `setup.sh --purge all` — stop/disable services, restore recorded basic
   settings (hostname, timezone, DNS, sysctl, PAM limits, governor, UFW,
   unattended-upgrades, cron).  Exit 0, 11 s, nothing deleted by the script.
2. `apt-get purge` of the 107 added packages.
3. Removal of test artifacts the purge intentionally preserves:
   `/var/www`, `/etc/angie`, `/etc/php`, `/etc/anubis`, `/etc/mysql`,
   `/var/lib/elasticsearch`, `/var/lib/setup.sh`, `/home/nginx_cache*`,
   `/etc/default/setup-tuning`, `/usr/local/bin/anubis`,
   `/etc/systemd/system/anubis.service`, the service drop-in directories,
   `/var/log/angie`, `/var/log/php8.*`, Angie/Sury/Elastic/NodeSource APT
   sources and keyrings, the 8 GB `/swapfile` and its fstab line, and the
   audit scratch trees.
4. Reboot.
